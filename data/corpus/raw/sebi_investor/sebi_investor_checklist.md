# SEBI Investor Checklist Snapshot

This local snapshot is a corpus seed for SEBI-style investor awareness material.

Use this source family for questions about:

- reading scheme documents before investing
- understanding risk disclosures and suitability
- not relying only on past returns
- checking costs, lock-in, liquidity, and product fit

This kind of source should be ranked highly when the assistant answers caution-heavy questions because regulatory investor guidance is safer than marketing-oriented copy.
